Unit 3: Society, Law & Ethics

Class 11 CS (Code 083) - Cyber Safety

1. Digital Footprints

Definition: Internet use karte waqt jo nishan hum chhodte hain (History, Cookies, Social Media Posts).

Two types:

  • Active: Jo data hum intentionally share karte hain (e.g. Instagram Post).
  • Passive: Jo data unknowingly collect hota hai (e.g. Browser History).

2. Cyber Crime & Safety

Online crimes aur unse bachne ke tareeke.

  • • Phishing: Fake emails bhejkar password churana.
  • • Hacking: Unauthorized access to computer.
  • • Cyber Bullying: Online kisi ko pareshan karna.
Safety Tip: Hamesha Strong Password use karein (Mix of letters, numbers, symbols) aur 2FA (Two-Factor Authentication) on rakhein.

3. IPR & E-waste

IPR (Intellectual Property Rights): Dimag se banaye gaye idea par haq (Copyright, Patent, Trademark).
E-waste Management: Purane electronic gadgets (Mobile, Laptop) ko sahi se dispose karna taaki environment ko nuksan na ho.
