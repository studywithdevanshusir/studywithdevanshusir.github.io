1. Networking Basics

Network: Jab do ya do se zyada computers aapas mein connect hote hain resources share karne ke liye (File, Printer).

Types of Networks:

  • LAN (Local Area Network): Ek room ya building tak seemit (e.g. School Lab).
  • MAN (Metropolitan Area Network): Ek shehar tak (Cable TV Network).
  • WAN (Wide Area Network): Poore world mein (Internet).
  • PAN (Personal Area Network): Personal range (Bluetooth).

2. Network Topologies

Computers ko connect karne ka layout/structure.

  • • Bus Topology: Ek single cable (backbone) se sab connected hote hain.
  • • Star Topology: Sab ek central Hub/Switch se connected hote hain (Most Common).
  • • Ring Topology: Circular loop mein twisted pair cable.
  • • Mesh Topology: Sab ek dusre se directly connected hote hain.

3. Network Protocols

Rules jo data transfer control karte hain.

Protocol Full Form Usage
HTTP/HTTPS HyperText Transfer Protocol (Secure) Webpages access karne ke liye
FTP File Transfer Protocol Files upload/download karne ke liye
SMTP Simple Mail Transfer Protocol Email bhejne ke liye
