Part A: Employability Skills

Unit 1: Communication Skills

Communication Cycle: Sender message bhejta hai -> Receiver usse samajhta hai -> Feedback wapas bhejta hai.

  • Verbal Communication: Bolkar (Oral) ya likhkar (Written) baat karna.
    Example: Phone call, Email, Letter.
  • Non-Verbal Communication: Bina bole, body language aur expressions se baat karna.
    Example: Smile karna (Happy), Haath hilana (Bye).
  • Visual Communication: Images aur signs ka use karna.
    Example: Traffic lights, Emoji.
Tip: Feedback dena communication ka sabse important part hai!
Unit 2: Self Management Skills

Self Management: Apne emotions, thoughts aur behavior ko control karna taaki goals achieve ho sakein.

  • Stress Management: Exam time par tension na lena, positive sochna.
  • Self-Awareness: Apni strengths (taakat) aur weaknesses (kamzori) ko pehchanna.
  • Self-Confidence: Khud par vishwas rakhna ki "Main kar sakta hoon!".
Skill: "Time Management" is key. Priority set karo aur distractions hatado.
Unit 3: ICT Skills

ICT (Information & Communication Technology): Computers aur internet ka use daily life mein.

  • Hardware vs Software:
    Hardware: Jisko touch kar sakte hain (Monitor, Keyboard).
    Software: Programs jo computer chalate hain (Windows, MS Word).
  • Basic Operations: File create karna, save karna, copy-paste karna.
  • Internet Safety: Strong password rakhna aur unknown links par click na karna.
Fact: CPU is the brain of the computer.
Unit 4: Entrepreneurial Skills

Entrepreneurship: Apna khud ka business shuru karna aur risk lena.

  • Qualities of an Entrepreneur:
    1. Patience (Sabr): Wait karna success ke liye.
    2. Creativity: Naya idea sochna.
    3. Hard Work: Mehnat karna.
  • Roles: Local economy ko boost karna aur jobs create karna.
Example: Ek chai ki tapri se bada cafe chain banana entrepreneurship hai.
Unit 5: Green Skills

Green Skills: Environment (Paryavaran) ko bachane ke tareeke sikhna.

  • Sustainable Development: Aisi taraqqi karna jo future generation ka nuksan na kare.
  • Environment Conservation: "Save Earth"
    - Ped lagana (Planting trees).
    - Pani bachana (Water conservation).
  • 3Rs Policy: Reduce (Kam use karo), Reuse (Dobara use karo), Recycle.
Action: Plastic bags band karke cloth bags use karna green skill hai.
