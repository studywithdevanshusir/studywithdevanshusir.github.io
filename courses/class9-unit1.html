Unit 1: Introduction to IT-ITeS Industry

Class 9 IT (Code 402)

1. IT & ITeS Kya Hai?

IT Definition: Information Technology (IT) ka matlab hai computers aur software ka use karke information ko create, process, store aur exchange karna.
ITeS Definition: ITeS (Information Technology Enabled Services) wo services hain jo IT ka use karti hain business ki efficiency badhane ke liye. Jaise Call Centers, Medical Transcription.

2. BPO Services (Business Process Outsourcing)

Jab koi company apna kaam (jaise customer support) kisi dusri company se karwati hai, usse BPO kehte hain. For example, agar ek US company apna call center India mein chalvati hai, toh wo BPO hai.

Types of BPO Companies:

  • MNCs (Multi-National Companies): Jo multiple countries mein operate karti hain.
  • ISPs (Indian Service Providers): Jo mainly India mein based hain.
  • GICs (Global In-house Centers): Parent company ke hi apne office dusri country mein.
